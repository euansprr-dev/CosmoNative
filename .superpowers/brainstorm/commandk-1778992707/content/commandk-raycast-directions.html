Decision 2 — Interaction model
What happens when you select a row?
Today a click opens immediately. Raycast’s “magic” is: selecting previews, Enter opens. This is the one real behavioral change.
↑↓ previewOpen ↵ · ⌘K

A. Select previews · Enter opens

Arrow/click highlights and live-previews on the right; Enter or the action-bar primary actually opens. The Raycast feel — fast browsing without committing. Requires decoupling select from open.

1-click preview · 2× open⌘K

B. Hybrid: 1-click preview, double/Enter open

Single click previews, double-click or Enter opens. Familiar Finder muscle memory, but double-click on a command surface can feel heavy and is less keyboard-pure.

hover = peek

C. Keep instant-open, peek on hover

Preserves today’s “click opens now”; preview only appears on hover/Space as a peek. Lowest risk & smallest diff, but you don’t get the calm browse-then-commit flow you liked.

Decision 3 — Preview emphasis
What does the right pane lead with?
Your items vary wildly: swipes have thumbnails, notes are text, ideas have hooks, books have covers.
INFORMATION

A. Adaptive per type

Each type leads with what matters — thumbnail for swipes/images, hooks for ideas, excerpt for notes — always followed by a compact “Information” table. Richest, most useful; most renderers to build.

B. Big visual first

Always lead with a large thumbnail/hero, minimal text beneath. Gorgeous for media-heavy libraries, weak for text notes and ideas (lots of faux-page placeholders).

INFORMATION

C. Reading excerpt + Information

Clipboard-manager style (like the Raycast shot): serif title, text excerpt, then the metadata table. Calmest and most “editorial”, but undersells visual swipe/image content.

My recommendation

1A · Spaces as a rail section — keeps everything keyboard-reachable and kills the dead-space problem outright. 2A · Select previews, Enter opens — this is the Raycast magic; it's a contained behavioral change (stop calling openSelected() on row tap; let ↵ / the action bar do it). 3A · Adaptive preview + Information table — your content is too varied for one fixed layout; the per-type renderers + a shared metadata table reuse the existing Spotlight* preview components and the per-browser preview panes you already have. Tell me if you'd rather swap any lane (e.g. 1C if you want it ruthlessly minimal).
