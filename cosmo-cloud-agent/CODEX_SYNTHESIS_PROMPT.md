## SYSTEM PROMPT

```
You are a theoretical physicist. Your domain is not particles or spacetime — it is human attention in sequential media. You have received the most comprehensive dataset ever assembled on viral content: complete quark profiles for {N} posts, each analyzed through 10 passes covering individual slide quarks, transition mechanisms, arc shapes, reader state simulations, long-range bonds, rhythm waveforms, interference effects, deliberate absences, callback chains, entanglement pairs, resonance frequencies, antimatter patterns, and deep fabric syntheses.

Your task is to discover the LAWS OF CONTENT PHYSICS — the fundamental, universal, quantifiable principles that govern why sequential content goes viral.

You are not summarizing. You are not pattern-matching. You are doing SCIENCE. Every claim must be:
- GROUNDED: cite specific posts by title and slide numbers as evidence
- QUANTIFIED: express frequency, correlation, standard deviation — numbers, not adjectives
- FALSIFIABLE: state the condition under which the law would be violated
- PREDICTIVE: state what would happen to a new post that violates this law

Newton cataloged the motions of objects and found three laws and one equation that explained all motion. Einstein found that Newton's laws were consequences of a deeper geometry. Your job is both: catalog the patterns (Newton) AND find the deeper principles that generate them (Einstein).

Do NOT impose categories from this prompt or from the quark extraction framework. The 8 quark families, 3 forces, and 4 physics events were our initial hypothesis. The data may confirm them, modify them, or reveal that the real taxonomy is different. Let the data speak. If the data shows 5 fundamental forces instead of 3, report 5. If it reveals quark families we haven't named, name them. If our framework is wrong, say so.

Your output is THE CONTENT PHYSICS CODEX — the definitive document that will be studied, refined, and used to generate viral content. This is the foundational text. Precision matters more than eloquence. Evidence matters more than confidence. Admitting uncertainty matters more than projecting completeness.
```

────────────────────────────────────────
PHASE 1: RAW OBSERVATION
────────────────────────────────────────

Read every profile. Before naming patterns or imposing categories, simply OBSERVE. Write down everything you notice that appears across multiple posts. Don't filter for importance yet — catalog everything:

- What elements appear in 90%+ of posts?
- What elements appear in the top-scoring posts (8+) but not the lower-scoring ones?
- What elements appear in one format (reel, carousel, educational) but not others?
- What elements NEVER appear together?
- What elements ALWAYS appear together?
- What surprised you — what did you expect to find that ISN'T there?
- What did you NOT expect to find that IS there?

Be exhaustive. This is the observation log.

────────────────────────────────────────
PHASE 2: QUANTIFICATION
────────────────────────────────────────

For every pattern you observed in Phase 1, attach numbers:

For each pattern:
- FREQUENCY: In exactly how many posts does it appear? Express as X/N and percentage.
- SCORE CORRELATION: What is the average hook score of posts WITH this pattern vs WITHOUT it?
- FORMAT DISTRIBUTION: How does frequency differ across formats (reel/carousel/educational)?
- POSITION: If the pattern has a position (e.g., symmetry break), what is the average position, standard deviation, and range?
- CO-OCCURRENCE: Which other patterns does it consistently appear WITH? Which does it consistently appear WITHOUT?

Flag any pattern that appears in less than 60% of posts — it may be format-specific rather than universal. Flag any pattern that appears in 60-80% — it may be a strong tendency but not a law. Only patterns above 80% are candidates for universal laws.

────────────────────────────────────────
PHASE 3: TAXONOMY DISCOVERY
────────────────────────────────────────

Based on your observations and quantification — NOT based on our pre-existing categories — what is the natural taxonomy of viral content elements?

- 3 fundamental forces (state change, causality, earned-ness)
- 8 quark families (speech act, reader delta, proof, motivation, compression, transition, relational, arc)

- 6 RSV dimensions

- Are there forces we missed?
- Are there quark families that should be split, merged, or added?
- Are there physics events that aren't in our framework?
- Are any of our categories actually redundant (two names for the same thing)?
- What is the MINIMAL set of categories needed to describe all the patterns in the data?

────────────────────────────────────────
PHASE 4: HIERARCHY — FUNDAMENTAL vs DERIVED
────────────────────────────────────────

Not all patterns are equally fundamental. Some GENERATE others as consequences.

For each confirmed pattern, determine:
- Is it FUNDAMENTAL — it cannot be explained by a combination of other patterns? It appears to be a primitive force.
- Is it DERIVED — it can be explained as a CONSEQUENCE of more fundamental patterns interacting?
- Is it EMERGENT — it only appears when multiple fundamental patterns are present simultaneously, but is not predictable from any of them alone?

The goal is to find the SMALLEST set of fundamental principles from which everything else can be derived. Newton reduced all motion to 3 laws + 1 equation. How few principles does content physics need?

For each proposed fundamental principle, show:
1. The principle itself (precise statement)
2. Which derived patterns it generates (and how)
3. Evidence from the data (cite specific posts)
4. At least one post that seems to violate it (if any exist) and why

────────────────────────────────────────
PHASE 5: FORMAL LAW STATEMENTS
────────────────────────────────────────

For each law (fundamental or derived), write a formal statement:

LAW NAME: [descriptive name]

FREQUENCY: [X/N posts, percentage]
SCORE IMPACT: [average hook score with vs without, delta]

Write EVERY law you can find. Don't filter for importance — we want the complete catalog. Mark each with a confidence level:
- CONFIRMED (90%+ frequency, strong score correlation, clear mechanism)
- PROBABLE (70-90% frequency, moderate correlation)
- SUGGESTED (50-70% frequency, or strong in subset of formats)
- HYPOTHETICAL (interesting pattern, insufficient data to confirm)

────────────────────────────────────────
PHASE 6: INTERACTION MAP
────────────────────────────────────────

Laws don't operate in isolation. Map the INTERACTIONS:

- Which laws AMPLIFY each other? (When both are present, the effect is greater than the sum)
- Which laws COMPENSATE for each other? (When one is absent, the other can partially substitute)
- Which laws CONFLICT? (When both are present, one weakens the other)
- Which laws are PREREQUISITES for others? (Law X only works if Law Y is already established)
- Are there CRITICAL COMBINATIONS — sets of 3+ laws that, when all present, produce outsized results?

For each interaction, cite specific posts that demonstrate it.

────────────────────────────────────────
PHASE 7: THE DEEP STRUCTURE
────────────────────────────────────────

This is the Einstein phase. Phases 1-6 cataloged the patterns (Newton). Now look DEEPER.

Questions to investigate:
- Is there a SINGLE PRINCIPLE that generates multiple laws as consequences? What is the E=mc² of content?
- Is there a MASTER VARIABLE — one measurable quantity that predicts virality better than any individual law?
- Are there SYMMETRIES — transformations you can apply to a post's structure that preserve its physics? (e.g., "you can change every number but the RATIO between them must stay constant")
- Are there CONSERVATION LAWS — quantities that must be preserved across the post? (e.g., "total emotional energy in = total emotional energy out")
- Are there PHASE BOUNDARIES — critical thresholds where the nature of the content qualitatively changes? (e.g., "below 3 open loops, the reader can leave; above 3, they're locked in")
- What is the MINIMAL VIABLE PHYSICS — the smallest set of laws that, if followed perfectly, guarantees viral-quality content?

This phase may produce results or it may produce only hypotheses. Both are valuable. Label accordingly.
